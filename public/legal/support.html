Support

Bridge by BloomingBox · Help for BloomingBox staff

Bridge is BloomingBox's internal workplace app. If you work at BloomingBox and something is not working, start here.

Contact

Email bridge@bloomingbox.com — we reply on working days, usually within one business day.
Account, access or HR questions: the People team at people@bloomingbox.com.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your work email and open the link we send you. The link works for 10 minutes and can be used once. If it has expired, request a new one.

I don't have an account / my account is inactive

Accounts are created by BloomingBox. Ask your manager or the People team to create or reactivate yours — there is no public sign-up.

Clock-in says I'm outside the geofence

Make sure Location is allowed for Bridge (iPhone: Settings → Bridge → Location → While Using the App, with Precise Location on). Move to an open area, wait a few seconds and try again. If you are genuinely at work and it still fails, clock in and submit a Fix a punch request so your manager can correct the time.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Bridge on your device, then in Bridge open More → Settings → Notifications and confirm the type you expect is switched on and that Do-Not-Disturb / quiet hours are not active.

Voice notes won't record

Allow Microphone access for Bridge in your device settings, then hold the mic button in the chat to record.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Open More → Delete my account in Bridge, or email the People team. See the Privacy Policy for what is deleted and what must be kept under UAE law.

Something looks wrong on my phone

Close and reopen the app (or hard-refresh the website). If it persists, send us a screenshot, your device model and the time it happened at bridge@bloomingbox.com.

Report a security concern

If you believe you have found a security issue in Bridge, please email bridge@bloomingbox.com with the details and do not share it elsewhere. We take every report seriously.
