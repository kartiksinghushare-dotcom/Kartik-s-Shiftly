Privacy Policy

Bridge by BloomingBox · Effective 21 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Bridge is the internal workplace application of BloomingBox Gifts LLC ("BloomingBox", "we", "us"), Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It is used only by BloomingBox employees and authorised contractors for daily work: shift checklists, attendance, OKRs, tickets, team chat and people records. This policy explains what personal data Bridge processes, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Bridge website (bridge.bloomingbox.com) and the Bridge mobile apps.

In short
  • Bridge is for BloomingBox staff only. Your account is created by BloomingBox, not by public sign-up.
  • We process your data to run the workplace: schedules, attendance, tasks, messages and HR records.
  • Location is used only while you clock in or out for attendance. Microphone is used only while you record a voice note. Nothing runs in the background.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can request account deletion from inside the app.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is BloomingBox Gifts LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Questions about this policy or your data go to the People team at people@bloomingbox.com, or through Support.

3. Why we process it

  • To perform your employment relationship — scheduling, attendance and time records, task assignment, performance objectives, approvals and HR administration, in line with the BloomingBox People Policies & Procedures Manual.
  • To run the workplace — team communication, ticket handling, checklists and reporting.
  • To keep Bridge secure — authentication, access control, audit logs, abuse and error monitoring.
  • To comply with UAE law — labour, tax, health-and-safety and record-keeping obligations.

Where UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (Personal Data Protection Law) applies, our legal bases are performance of the employment contract, compliance with legal obligations and BloomingBox's legitimate interest in operating its business securely. Device features (location, microphone, camera, photos, notifications) are used only after you grant permission on your device, and you can withdraw that permission at any time in your device settings.

5. Who can see your data

Bridge is role-based. Your colleagues see your name, photo, position and what you post in shared channels. Your manager sees your attendance, checklists, objectives and requests. The People team and system administrators see HR records. Compensation fields, where used, are restricted to super administrators and the Head of People. Private messages are visible only to their participants.

We share data with service providers who process it on our behalf under contract and our instructions:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage for Bridge.
  • Vercel — hosting of the web application.
  • Email and push delivery providers — to send notification emails and push messages you have opted into.
  • Apple and Google — app distribution and push notification services on iOS and Android.

We may also disclose data where required by law, a court or a competent UAE authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of BloomingBox and its people. We never sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. International transfers

Our providers may store data on servers outside the UAE. Where that happens we rely on contractual safeguards with the provider and on the security measures described below.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and HR records: for the duration of your employment and afterwards for as long as UAE labour, tax and audit rules require (typically up to five years after exit, per the BloomingBox People Audit policy).
  • Attendance, checklists, tickets and objectives: retained as business records for the same period; they remain visible to authorised managers for reporting.
  • Messages and voice notes: retained while the channel exists; you can delete your own messages, and administrators can archive channels.
  • Location data: only the clock-in / clock-out point is stored, with the attendance record.
  • Sign-in logs and audit trail: up to 12 months.

8. Security

Bridge uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), password-based sign-in with time-limited password-reset links, row-level access rules in the database so each person only reads what their role allows, private file storage with expiring links, and an audit log of administrative actions. Access to production systems is limited to named administrators. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will inform you and the relevant authority as required.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — most profile fields can be edited in Bridge; for anything else, contact the People team.
  • Deletion — you can request deletion of your account from More → Delete my account in the app, or by email. We will deactivate the account promptly and delete or anonymise personal data that we are not legally required to keep. Records that UAE law obliges us to retain (for example payroll and attendance history) are kept for the statutory period and then deleted.
  • Notifications — turn each type on or off in Settings, or in your device's notification settings.
  • Device permissions — revoke location, microphone, camera or photo access at any time in your device settings; the related feature will simply stop working.
  • Complaints — raise a concern with the Head of People or the Managing Partners through the confidential channels described in the People Manual, or with the UAE Data Office.

10. Children

Bridge is a workplace tool. BloomingBox does not employ anyone under 18, and Bridge is not intended for or offered to children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Bridge or the law changes. The version and effective date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced inside Bridge.
